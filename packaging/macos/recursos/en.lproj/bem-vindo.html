Nexus Modeler

Capture quality with the ease of modeling.

Free and open source guitar plugin. This installer places:

  • VST3 plugin in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • AU plugin (Audio Unit, for Logic and GarageBand) in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  • Standalone app (no recording software needed) in /Applications

In the Installation Type step you can uncheck anything you don't want.

The Nexus screen comes in 15 languages. The first time, it opens in your Mac's language; to change it, click the globe in the corner of the screen.
